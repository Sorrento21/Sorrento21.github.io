刘 佳 成

I design sensing systems that live on the body — quiet instruments that notice what a person cannot, and help before it is asked for.

First-year Ph.D. student at the SciFi Lab, Cornell University, advised by Prof. Cheng Zhang. My research centers on AI-assisted sensing technologies with an emphasis on wearable form factors: activity-detection systems and assistive devices offering unobtrusive, seamless monitoring and support.

Before Cornell I earned a B.Eng. in Creative Design and Intelligent Engineering at Xinya College, Tsinghua University, with research in the PI Lab and the Future Lab.
