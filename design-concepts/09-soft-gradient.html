👋 Hi, I'm Jiacheng — first-year Ph.D. at Cornell

Making sensing gentle enough to live with.刘佳成

I'm at the SciFi Lab, Cornell University, advised by Prof. Cheng Zhang. My research centers on AI-assisted sensing technologies with an emphasis on wearable form factors — activity-detection systems and assistive devices.

See my research Download CV
Wearable sensing

Research question

How can we integrate AI with sensing systems to develop early-detection systems and assistive devices for everyday use — providing unobtrusive, seamless health support for individuals with diverse health conditions?
