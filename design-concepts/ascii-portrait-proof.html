proof · character art straight from your photo

No image model needed — this is your actual photo, resampled to characters

ffmpeg resamples the photo to a character grid, each cell picks a glyph by ink density, and the background is cut away below a brightness threshold. Colour comes from the theme, so it recolours itself; it is text, so it stays sharp at any zoom. Three densities below — the wider the grid, the more the face survives.
