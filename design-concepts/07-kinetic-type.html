I build sensing systems small enough to forget you are wearing them.

First-year Ph.D. student at the SciFi Lab, Cornell University, advised by Prof. Cheng Zhang. My research centers on AI-assisted sensing technologies with an emphasis on wearable form factors — activity-detection systems and assistive devices for unobtrusive, seamless monitoring and support.

Previously B.Eng. in Creative Design and Intelligent Engineering, Xinya College, Tsinghua University.
