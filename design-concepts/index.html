Personal site · redesign options · round 2

Ten directions

Five kept from round one and reworked, five brand new. Every option is a complete working homepage built from your real content, publications, project shots and paintings. Click a card to open it full size; resize the window to check the responsive behaviour. Nothing here touches the live site — these are standalone prototypes in website/design-concepts/.

1 · Images lead

Every paper and project is now an image card first — figures are large, croppable and open full-size in a lightbox. Each media slot also accepts a GIF or looping video.

2 · Motion & interaction

Scroll reveals, parallax, drag-scroll rails, hover previews, keyboard navigation. Every effect degrades gracefully and respects prefers-reduced-motion.

3 · Strong key visual, still usable

One deliberate hero idea per concept — never at the cost of legibility, and never blocking the path to the CV, the papers or the contact details.

Previews blank, or "Open full page" not working? This page needs a local server running — the concepts load images from ../src/images/. Double-click design-concepts/preview.command in Finder: it starts the server, opens this page, and keeps running until you close its Terminal window (Ctrl-C).

11Cinematicnew

A full-viewport portrait with a slow Ken Burns drift opens the page, then each paper takes an entire screen-height band: its figure fills the background at 60% brightness and moves at a different speed than the text as you scroll. Reads like film titles. Projects close it out as a mosaic.

Key visual
Full-bleed hero + full-width paper bands
Interaction
Ken Burns · scroll parallax · progress bar · lightbox
Risk
Long page; the figures must survive heavy cropping

12Filmstripnew

Papers live in a horizontal strip of large 16:9 cards you drag, wheel, arrow-key or step through with buttons; the centred card is highlighted and a dot row tracks position. Light warm paper background keeps it academic — the motion is in the strip, not in the page.

Key visual
Big horizontal cards on warm paper
Interaction
Snap scroll · drag · wheel-to-pan · arrow keys · lightbox
Risk
Horizontal scrolling is not everyone's instinct

13Media Wallnew

Everything you have made on one masonry wall — papers, design projects and paintings mixed, filtered by Research / Design / Art, with a couple of text panels woven in. Hovering dims everything else; clicking opens a lightbox with the full figure, the authors and the venue, and ← → walks the whole set.

Key visual
A single wall of everything you have made
Interaction
Filters · staggered entrance · hover dim · lightbox with prev/next
Risk
Flattens the hierarchy — a paper and a sketch look equally weighted

14Spotlightnew

The hero is a live canvas: a field of dots with a pulse wave travelling through it — your own subject matter as the key visual — and the dots push away from your cursor. Below, a warm spotlight follows the pointer inside each paper card. The animation pauses itself when scrolled out of view.

Key visual
Interactive sensor field (canvas)
Interaction
Cursor-reactive particles · per-card spotlight · reveal · lightbox
Risk
The only concept whose hero needs JS; canvas costs a little battery

15Card Decknew

Papers are a physical deck: the top card shows one big figure, you flick it away with the mouse (or arrow keys, or the buttons) and the next slides up, while the panel beside it swaps the number, venue and a one-sentence summary. Clicking a card opens the figure full size.

Key visual
Stacked, throwable cards
Interaction
Drag-to-dismiss · keyboard · progress bar · lightbox
Risk
Only one paper visible at a time; needs a plain list elsewhere
